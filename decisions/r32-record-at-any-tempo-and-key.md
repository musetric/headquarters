# R-32. Singing and recording work at any tempo and key

**Decided** 2026-10-05.

## Decision

A user can sing and record at any tempo and in any key of the song. Nothing
that was sung is lost, and what a change of tempo or key does to a take is
clear without an explanation.

## Why

Slowing down, singing and speeding up again is the core of practice, and the
earlier ban on recording at a changed tempo or key was a technical limit, not
a choice. Hardly any singing app records at a slowed-down tempo.
