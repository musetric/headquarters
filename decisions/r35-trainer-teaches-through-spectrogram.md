# R-35. The trainer teaches through the spectrogram, not with a score

**Decided** 2026-10-11.

## Decision

The trainer leads an amateur the way a teacher does, through the spectrogram.
It finds the hard phrases of a song, shows on the spectrogram the one place
where the student sang differently from the singer, the two side by side,
lets the student hear it, and has them sing exactly that phrase again. A
concept of the picture, such as pitch, timing, breath, gestures or vibrato, is
explained once, the first time it appears in the student's singing.

It never gives a score, a percentage or a similarity in place of the picture
and an action, and it shows no hints or red verdicts while the student sings.

## Why

An amateur cannot judge their own singing, even from a recording, and neither
a raw spectrogram nor a number says what to change, while one explained goal
on a short piece does. The student grows the way a professional does, by
learning to read the sound.
