# Paid work

How members take paid work, within the charter's
[How members earn](../charter.md#how-members-earn) and
[Independent](../charter.md#independent).

- **Expertise.** Consulting and contracts draw on what the project made its
  members expert in, such as correct and fast neural networks on ordinary
  devices, WebGPU and mobile GPUs.
- **Commissioned work** is a feature an organization needs, support with a
  response time over a school year, compliance (accessibility, protection of
  students' data, localization), deployment, long-term support of a version,
  or training for teachers.
- **Sponsorship and donations pay for what the project needs to run**, such
  as signing certificates, domains and test devices.
- **A contract with an institution goes through a fiscal host or a partner
  company** that takes the payment and answers for continuity, because an
  institution cannot depend on one person.
- **A contract promises a response time, not a time to fix.** A defect of a
  browser or a driver is worked around where possible; its fix is not ours
  to promise.
- **A customer's or a regulator's requirement is met with a setting or a
  profile of the one product**, never with a separate version for one
  customer or one country.
