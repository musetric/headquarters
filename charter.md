# The Musetric charter

Who we are, what we make and why, what we will and will not do, and how the
people who work on Musetric earn a living. The charter binds every member of
the organization, its owner included, and anyone who speaks for the project.

## Mission

- Education in music and singing should be accessible first, professional,
  and in step with its time, so that everyone can receive it, whatever their
  circumstances.
- The organization raises the accessibility of this education, and raises
  its quality without trading accessibility away.
- Every contribution is valuable. What people create together remains a
  common good and serves people.

## What we make

Musetric makes free, professional tools for practising music and singing.
The source code is open, under the MIT license.

## On the user's own device

Everything is computed on the user's own device. That is a choice, made for
three reasons:

- **Privacy.** Whoever wants full confidentiality has it: nothing of theirs
  needs to leave the device.
- **Free without a server bill.** No rented servers to pay for is the surest
  way to keep the product free on little or no funding.
- **Others can build on it.** Anyone can take the code and the experience
  and try something of their own without depending on a Musetric server,
  because nothing runs through one.

It is not a vow to stay offline forever. Online services, free or paid, may
come where they serve people, such as lessons between a teacher and a
student. They never move to the centre of the product or the organization:
the mission holds with no funding or with little, depends on no exclusive
arrangement, and never stops anyone outside the organization from building
something of their own on this experience.

## Quality and simplicity

- **Quality comes first**: the best result and the best experience the
  project can give, shared with the world.
- **Simple as a whole.** The whole product is simple to use and to
  understand. Quality and new capabilities do not arrive as more and more
  settings: rather than leaving every choice to the user, the product looks
  for the solution that works for most people.
- **Fewer, more general solutions.** Problems that share a grain are solved
  by one concept, clear enough for many people to understand.
- **Hard inside, simple outside.** A concept may be deep and hard to build;
  that difficulty is ours to carry, never a reason for a more complicated
  product.

## Free, forever

- The app carries no money: what it does on the user's own device is free,
  with no ads, no subscriptions, no paid features, no paid builds or paid
  versions in the stores, and the app is not sold.
- A future online service may have a price only as "On the user's own
  device" allows, and never by taking away what the app gives for free.
- The name and the logo are
  [reserved](https://github.com/musetric/musetric/blob/main/trademark.md), so
  that nobody can sell a paid or harmful copy under the project's name. Forks
  are welcome under a name of their own.

## Independent

- No investors and no directors.
- No money that comes with control over the product: no exclusive sponsorship
  terms, no say over the roadmap in exchange for funding.
- One product for everyone: no separate version for one customer or one
  country.
- A feature is built because the people who learn need it, and it is given to
  everyone.

## How members earn

Members earn from their own work and expertise, never from access to the
product: consulting and contracts in what the project made them expert in,
work that an organization commissions and pays for, grants for open
software, culture and education, sponsorship and donations, and content,
talks and workshops. What is built for pay enters the free product for
everyone: the payment buys work and obligations, not access and not
control, and no money restricts any user.

Paid work never crowds out the product.

## The users' trust

- No silent telemetry. Anything the app collects is opt-in, anonymous and
  never contains the user's content.
- No user's data and no track that is not openly licensed is ever published.
  What may come out of them is only what serves others: findings and
  anonymous statistics that share the project's experience and expertise
  with other people and organizations, with nothing in them that identifies a
  user or a track.

## How we work

- **In the open.** Problems, decisions, plans and measurements are public,
  so that anyone can see what is done and why; material that may not be
  published stays out of them.
- **Members answer for the whole of what they own.** Before changing
  something, a member understands why it is as it is, whom it serves and
  what for, and solves that problem rather than the text of a request.
- **One truth.** A contradiction between what is written, what is built and
  what is real is resolved before the work goes on.
- [practices/](practices/readme.md) is how this charter is applied day to
  day.
